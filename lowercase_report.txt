during the morning inspection, the operations team noticed an unusual sound coming from the conveyor system.
the maintenance team was informed immediately and the conveyor was temporarily shut down.
a technician found that one of the rollers was damaged and required replacement.
the nearby loading area remained operational, although movement was slower than usual.
later in the afternoon, the damaged roller was replaced and the conveyor resumed operation.
the team reported that normal operations resumed at 4:30 pm.
no injuries were reported during the incident.
a follow-up inspection has been scheduled for next week.
